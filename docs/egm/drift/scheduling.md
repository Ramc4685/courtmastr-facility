## backend/app/modules/scheduling/README.md

- [contract] Line 15 says the router serves "31 operations of `openapi-core.yaml` tag `scheduling`, plus 8 from phase 10" (39 in total). `router.py` declares 40 routes (`backend/app/modules/scheduling/router.py:41-321`). 37 of them match `openapi-core.yaml` operations tagged `scheduling`, including the phase 10 grants (`openapi-core.yaml:3266,3280`) and the slot-offer list, create and update (`openapi-core.yaml:3141,3180,3203`). So "plus 8 from phase 10" does not count operations outside the contract.
- [contract] The three routes that lacked a contract operation now have one: `updateBookingSeriesRule` (`PATCH /booking-series/{booking_series_id}/rules/{rule_id}`, `openapi-core.yaml:2789`) and `listSlotOfferSlots` / `bookSlotOfferSlot` (`/slot-offers/{slot_offer_id}/slots`, `openapi-core.yaml:3295`). Line 15's count still predates them.
- [undocumented] `__init__.py:12` registers `service.check_location_archive` with `platform.service.register_location_archive_check`: archiving a location with a confirmed booking still to start is refused with 409 `location-has-future-bookings` (`service.py`, `check_location_archive`). The README's interface block (lines 19-30) does not mention it.
- [contract] `openapi-core.yaml` has eight scheduling-tagged operations with no route. The permission key `scheduling.public_schedule.manage` is registered (`permissions.py:42`) but has no route.

The context publishes `scheduling.booking.created`, `.rescheduled` and `.cancelled`, `scheduling.hold.expired`, `scheduling.session.created`, `.cancelled` and `.capacity_changed`, `scheduling.series.generated`, `scheduling.slot_offer.saved` and `scheduling.reservation_type.grants_replaced` (`events.py:20-81`; published from `service.py` and `offers.py`). Engagement and staff consume the booking events.
