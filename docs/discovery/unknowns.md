### Trade-offs for the architect
- Class vs camp duality: the reference product runs "classes" as package-consuming calendar sessions and "camps/clinics/programs" as fee-based Groups with separate registration, waitlist and reporting. Copying that yields two enrolment engines. Option: one Enrolment model with two pricing strategies (entitlement vs fee) on top of the same reservation engine; cost is divergence from parity vocabulary. Evidence: https://support.ezfacility.com/hc/en-us/articles/4402427209115-Registering-A-Client-Into-a-Single-Available-Session, https://www.ezfacility.com/features/member-engagement
- Reservation vs billing decoupling: rentals can exist unbilled, be billed later in arbitrary groupings, and be routed to existing invoices. Flexible but creates revenue leakage and awkward cancellation. Option: keep decoupling for staff (parity) but require a charge decision state on every rental occurrence and surface an unbilled queue. Evidence: https://support.ezfacility.com/hc/en-us/articles/4407310264475-How-to-Add-Additional-Rentals-to-an-Existing-Rental-in-EZFacility-Classic, https://support.ezfacility.com/hc/en-us/articles/4404308503195-How-to-Schedule-a-Rental-Reservation-in-EZFacility-Classic
- Trainer as resource: availability is modelled as open sessions and blocks on the trainer column, not as working-hours rules. For the exclusion constraint this argues for trainers being rows in the same resource table (or a polymorphic allocation target). Evidence: https://support.ezfacility.com/hc/en-us/articles/4404311260571-How-to-Schedule-an-Available-Session-in-EZFacility-Classic, https://support.ezfacility.com/hc/en-us/articles/4404311270683-Scheduling-a-Special-Reservation-in-EZFacility-Classic
- Split sessions: materialising every slot as a reservation is simple and constraint-friendly but explodes row counts for long recurring availability (e.g. 15-minute slots, daily, one year, per trainer). Alternative is availability windows with slots derived at query time and bookings as the only constrained rows. Recommend the latter for trainer availability and materialised occurrences for classes. Evidence: https://support.ezfacility.com/hc/en-us/articles/4404311260571-How-to-Schedule-an-Available-Session-in-EZFacility-Classic, https://support.ezfacility.com/hc/en-us/articles/36516396025115-April-23rd-2025-Release-Notes
- Online checkout holds: nothing public describes how a slot is held during payment. With Stripe asynchronous confirmation we need a hold row inside the exclusion constraint with TTL and an expiry job; idempotent webhook confirms or releases. ## Billing & Invoicing, Payments & Merchant

### Ledger semantics (architect attention)

- Three distinct "undo" operations exist and are easy to conflate: reverse an item (negates revenue, optional back-dating), void an invoice (only ungroups items), void an item (deletes from the books, irreversible), plus void payment vs refund on the money side. None of the articles states the accounting entries. Assumption: immutable double-sided ledger where reversal = negating entry, invoice void = status change, item void = privileged hard cancel that is still retained in audit. Trade-off: their model allows mutable edits of price/discount on paid items; we must decide whether edits on paid charges create adjustment entries. Evidence: https://support.ezfacility.com/hc/en-us/articles/4402433807515-How-to-Void-an-Invoice, https://support.ezfacility.com/hc/en-us/articles/4402433754267-How-to-Reverse-the-Billing-of-an-Item, https://support.ezfacility.com/hc/en-us/articles/4402426355099-How-to-Void-a-Payment, https://support.ezfacility.com/hc/en-us/articles/25737423648923-How-to-Partially-Refund-a-Sale

Assumption: store all three dates; recognition schedule is a reporting concern. ## Leagues & Tournaments, Groups & Online Registration

### Architecture trade-offs
- Three products vs one. The source has a core app (groups), a separately authenticated league app with its own users/roles/players, and a form product; data moves by one-way "publish" and SSO deep links. Assumption: we build one model (program -> competition -> teams -> roster of core clients) and drop the publish step, the separate player database and the separate role set. Trade-off: loses nothing functionally but REG-14/LGE-26 become thin. Evidence: https://support.ezfacility.com/hc/en-us/articles/4405044226587-How-to-Publish-a-Group-to-EZLeagues-from-EZFacility, https://support.ezfacility.com/hc/en-us/articles/22122496577179-January-17th-2024-Release-Notes.
- League games vs facility bookings. The generator's "Avoid Double Booking Facilities" is an opt-in checkbox and the league app has its own "locations"; nothing public states that generated games block the main facility calendar (a 2023 fix mentions an "EZLeagues Resource View" whose resource order follows subgroup order, hinting games surface in the core calendar). Assumption: every committed game is a booking under the exclusion constraint, so the checkbox is always-on; the generator must therefore read existing bookings as hard constraints and report unschedulable games instead of failing the transaction. Evidence: https://support.ezfacility.com/hc/en-us/articles/26753867674011-How-to-Generate-a-League-Schedule-Even-Number-of-Team, https://support.ezfacility.com/hc/en-us/articles/17981009155995-August-16th-2023-Release-Notes.
