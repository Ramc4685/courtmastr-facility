## backend/app/modules/scheduling/README.md

- [contract] Line 15 says the router serves "31 operations of `openapi-core.yaml` tag `scheduling`, plus 8 from phase 10" (39 in total). `router.py` declares 40 routes (`backend/app/modules/scheduling/router.py:41-321`). 37 of them match `openapi-core.yaml` operations tagged `scheduling`, including the phase 10 grants (`openapi-core.yaml:3266,3280`) and the slot-offer list, create and update (`openapi-core.yaml:3141,3180,3203`). The other three have contract operations as well (`updateBookingSeriesRule`, `openapi-core.yaml:2789`; `listSlotOfferSlots` and `bookSlotOfferSlot`, `openapi-core.yaml:3297,3318`), so the count of 39 matches neither the 40 routes nor the contract.
- [stale-not-built] "Not built" (lines 104-107) lists the public schedule (SCH-20) and calendar feeds (SCH-19) as missing. They are built, in the integrations context: `GET` and `PATCH /public-schedule-settings` are tagged `scheduling` and gated by `scheduling.public_schedule.manage` (`backend/app/modules/integrations/router.py:362,368`; `permissions.py:42`), and `feeds.py` reads bookings through `scheduling.service` (`backend/app/modules/integrations/feeds.py:371-373`). That leaves five scheduling-tagged operations with no route anywhere: `createSessionDirectLink`, `revokeSessionDirectLink`, `createSlotOfferDirectLink` and `resolveDirectLink` (SCH-16), plus `mergeBookingSeries` (SCH-21) (`openapi-core.yaml:3416-3506`). The README maps none of them to operationIds.

The README's interface block (lines 17-30) does not list `booking_ids` at all, so the filter and the rest of its keywords (`client_id`, `client_ids`, `status`, `series_id`, `resource_id`, `starts_from`, `starts_to`) are undocumented.
