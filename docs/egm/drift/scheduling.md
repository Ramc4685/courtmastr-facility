## backend/app/modules/scheduling/README.md

- [contract] Line 15 says the router serves "31 operations of `openapi-core.yaml` tag `scheduling`, plus 8 from phase 10" (39 in total). `router.py` declares 43 routes (`backend/app/modules/scheduling/router.py:41-339`). 37 of them match `openapi-core.yaml` operations tagged `scheduling`, including the phase 10 grants (`openapi-core.yaml:3267,3281`) and the slot-offer list, create and update (`openapi-core.yaml:3142,3181,3204`). Three more are the virtual-session routes (see the video-meeting bullet), and three have no contract operation. So "plus 8 from phase 10" does not add up to the routes that exist.
- [contract] Three routes have no operation in any `docs/api/*.yaml`. They are `PATCH /booking-series/{booking_series_id}/rules/{rule_id}` (`router.py:244`), whose path appears in the contract only with `get` (`openapi-core.yaml:2738`), and `GET` and `POST /slot-offers/{slot_offer_id}/slots` (`router.py:333,339`). Lines 15 and 87 present them as built operations without noting that the contract lacks them.
- [contract] `openapi-core.yaml` has eight scheduling-tagged operations with no route: `createSessionDirectLink` (`openapi-core.yaml:3296`), `revokeSessionDirectLink` (3310), `createSlotOfferDirectLink` (3325), `resolveDirectLink` (3340), `getPublicSchedule` (3357), `getPublicScheduleSettings` (3367), `updatePublicScheduleSettings` (3376) and `mergeBookingSeries` (3386). "Not built" (lines 104-107) covers the first five and the merge in prose, but maps none of them to an operationId. The two `/public-schedule-settings` operations are not named anywhere in the README, and the permission key `scheduling.public_schedule.manage` is registered (`permissions.py:42`) with no route.
- [contract] The virtual-session section (lines 121-123) does not match the contract it implements. `openapi-programs.yaml:7108-7155` defines `GET`, `PUT` and `DELETE /sessions/{session_id}/video-meeting` (`getSessionVideoMeeting`, `putSessionVideoMeeting`, `deleteSessionVideoMeeting`, tag `integrations`), all gated by `scheduling.session.manage`. `PUT` takes a `VideoMeetingInput` body (`provider` manual or zoom, `join_url`, `passcode`) and every call returns `VideoMeeting` (`openapi-programs.yaml:9304-9335`). The code serves `/sessions/{schedule_session_id}/meeting` (`router.py:295-312`), `PUT` takes no body, the response is `SessionMeetingOut` (`schemas.py:236-245`, no `id`, `status` or `has_passcode`), and `GET` is gated by the scheduling read key (`service.py:1931`), as README line 122 says. The README states the code's behaviour but not that it departs from the contract on path, body, response and `GET` permission.
