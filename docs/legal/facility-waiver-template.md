# Facility Waiver, Assumption of Risk and Release (Template for Facilities)

DRAFT, not legal advice, pending attorney review. Last updated: 2026-11-13.

TEMPLATE ONLY. Whether and how a waiver or release is enforceable, especially for negligence, for minors, and for gross negligence or willful conduct, differs a great deal from state to state, and some states do not enforce releases signed by or for children, or require particular wording or conspicuous type. The facility's own lawyer licensed in the facility's state must adapt, approve and maintain this document before it is used. Marvy Labs LLC does not give legal advice, is not a party to any waiver between a facility and its members, and makes no statement that this wording is valid anywhere. Replace every bracketed item.

# [FACILITY NAME] Waiver, Assumption of Risk and Release

PLEASE READ CAREFULLY. [Facility's lawyer to confirm required notice language, type size and signature layout.]

**Participant** (name): [____] **Date of birth**: [____]

**If the participant is under 18**: parent or legal guardian name: [____]. In this document, "I" and "me" mean the adult signing, both for myself (if I am a participant) and on behalf of the minor participant.

## 1. Activities

I want to take part, or to allow the minor to take part, in activities at [FACILITY LEGAL NAME] ("the Facility"), including [court and room use, rentals, drop-in play, open gym, programs, classes, coaching, leagues, tournaments, camps, use of equipment, use of the building when unattended] (the "Activities").

## 2. Assumption of risk

2.1 I understand the Activities involve risks, including [falls, collisions with people, walls, nets and equipment, being struck by balls or equipment, strains, sprains, fractures, head injuries, heat illness, overexertion, slippery or uneven surfaces, equipment failure, the conduct of other participants, and in rare cases serious injury, permanent disability or death].

2.2 The Facility may be open without staff on site, which means help in an emergency may be delayed. I understand I must call emergency services myself for an emergency.

2.3 I understand there are other risks I cannot foresee. I choose to take part and accept these risks voluntarily.

## 3. Release and waiver of claims

3.1 [Facility's lawyer to draft for the Facility's state, and to confirm whether it may cover negligence, and the scope for minors.] To the fullest extent the law allows, I release and agree not to sue the Facility, its owners, officers, employees, coaches, volunteers, landlords, and its software and service providers (together, the "Released Parties") for any injury, illness, death, loss or damage arising out of or related to the Activities or my or the minor's presence at the Facility, including claims based on the Released Parties' ordinary negligence.

3.2 This release does not apply to claims the law does not allow me to release. [Facility's lawyer to confirm, for example for gross negligence, recklessness or willful misconduct.]

3.3 This release binds me, my heirs, executors, family members and assigns. [For minors: Facility's lawyer to confirm effect of a parent's release on the child's own claims in the Facility's state, and whether a court's approval or a different form is required.]

## 4. Indemnity (optional)

[Facility's lawyer to decide whether to include and to adapt to the state's law: I agree to indemnify the Released Parties against claims brought by or for the participant, or by others, arising from the participant's conduct, to the extent the law allows.]

## 5. Health, fitness and rules

5.1 I confirm the participant is physically able to take part, has no medical condition that makes participation unsafe except as I have told the Facility, and has consulted a doctor where appropriate.

5.2 I will follow the rules, staff instructions and posted signs, and the entry rules for unmanned use: one code per booking, no sharing of codes, no tailgating, minors supervised as the Facility requires. I understand the Facility may remove me or the participant for breach.

5.3 Medical information: [what the Facility asks for, how it is kept, and who sees it]. In an emergency I authorize the Facility to call emergency services and to give them emergency information, and for the minor, to arrange emergency treatment if I cannot be reached. [Facility's lawyer to confirm.]
