# Courtmastr Facility Privacy Policy

DRAFT, not legal advice, pending attorney review. Last updated: 2026-11-13.

This policy explains how Marvy Labs LLC ("Marvy Labs", "we", "us"), an Illinois limited liability company, handles personal information in connection with Courtmastr Facility (the "Service") and our website. Courtmastr Facility is business software. Facility operators ("Customers") use it to run courts, rooms, memberships, programs, leagues and other activities. Their members and guests ("End Users") use the portal and mobile app that the Customer offers.

## 1. Two roles, and which part of this policy applies to you

1.1 End User data. For personal information about End Users that a facility puts into or collects through the Service (bookings, memberships, payments records, waivers, check-ins, messages and so on), the facility is the controller or "business". It decides why and how the data is used. Marvy Labs acts as its processor or "service provider" and handles the data only on the facility's documented instructions, under a data processing agreement. The facility's own privacy notice governs that data. If you are an End User and want to see, correct, export or delete your data, or opt out of messages, contact the facility first. We will help the facility respond (see Section 9).

1.2 Our own data. For information we collect for ourselves, we are the controller or business. This covers: visitors to our website; people who contact us or ask for a demo; Customer staff who hold logins (we handle their identity and security data both for the Customer and to protect the Service); billing and contract contacts; and security and usage logs of the Service.

1.3 The sections below describe both, and say which role applies.

## 2. What we collect

### 2.1 Data processed for Customers about End Users

The data depends on what the facility chooses to use and configure. It can include:

(a) identity and contact details: name, email, phone, postal address, date of birth (age band is shown in lists rather than the date), photo, emergency contacts and household and guardian relationships;

(b) children's data: profile and date of birth of a child, entered by a parent or guardian or by staff (see our Children's Privacy Notice);

(c) bookings, rentals, memberships, packages, program and league registrations, rosters, results and attendance;

(d) payment records: Stripe customer and payment method identifiers, card brand, last four digits and expiry, bank name, invoices, charges, refunds and balances. We do not receive or store full card numbers, security codes or full bank account numbers;

(e) waivers and signed documents, including the signed text, the signature image, signer name, time, IP address and browser details;

(f) check-in and entry data: when a person checked in or out, the method (credential, desk, kiosk, app card), the result, and, where the facility uses door entry features, the booking-linked entry code issued, the time of door release, and entry and exit events. Credentials such as membership numbers, barcodes or RFID identifiers are stored. We do not store biometric templates;

(g) messages: the content, recipient, template and delivery status of emails, push notifications and (if enabled by the facility) text messages, plus communication consent records;

(h) health-related information the facility chooses to collect, such as injury notes or medical form answers, which the Service treats as sensitive;

(i) notes and attachments entered by staff.

We do not intentionally collect government identifiers, full card or bank numbers, biometric templates, precise device location or advertising identifiers, and the mobile app does not read contacts or photo libraries.

### 2.2 Data we collect for ourselves

(a) Staff and account data: name, work email, role, hashed password (we never store the password itself), multi-factor sign-in settings, sessions, IP address and browser details, and audit records of actions taken in the Service.

(b) Customer and billing contacts, contract and support records, and what people tell us when they contact us.

(c) Service Data: server and application logs that identify requests by technical identifiers (we aim to keep personal information out of logs), error reports and performance data.

(d) Website data: pages visited and basic request data. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking scripts in the Service. [TO CONFIRM: whether the marketing website will use any analytics or cookies; if so list them here and add a cookie notice.]

## 5. Retention

Facilities control how long they keep End User data, within the limits of the Service. Our default practices today are:

| Data | Default retention |
|---|---|
| Sign-in sessions | About 90 days after expiry or revocation |
| Single-use sign-in and reset tokens | About 30 days after expiry or use |
| Audit trail of actions in the Service | 24 months online, with a longer class retained up to 7 years |
| Message bodies (email, push and text content) | 90 days, after which only delivery records and counts remain [TO CONFIRM: purge for message bodies verified working before launch] |
| Data export files | A few days after creation, then deleted |
| Provider webhook records | Payload emptied after 90 days |
| Backups | Encrypted backups kept for a limited period [TO CONFIRM: backup retention; design target 35 days, scripts default to keeping the newest 14] |
| Signed waivers and documents | Kept for the period the facility sets (default 7 years) because they may be needed to defend legal claims. If the signer was a minor, the period runs from the age of majority |
| Billing records, invoices and ledger entries | Kept, linked to an anonymized profile after deletion, for accounting and tax obligations |

When an End User's data is deleted at the facility's approval, personal fields are removed or anonymized, and billing, attendance counts and accounting records remain in anonymized form. When a Customer ends its subscription we export its data on request and delete its tenant data 30 days after termination. Backup copies age out on the backup schedule. ## 6. Security

We use measures including separation of each facility's data enforced inside the database, role-based access with an audit trail, multi-factor sign-in for privileged roles, encryption in transit, encrypted backups, file scanning and a rule that card numbers are never stored. Our Security Overview describes what is in place and what is not yet. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Cookies and similar technologies

## 9. Your rights and choices

9.1 Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what we hold about you, access it, correct it, delete it, receive a portable copy, restrict or object to some uses, withdraw consent, and opt out of sale or targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of on those points.

9.2 For End User data, send requests to the facility. The Service has a request workflow: staff log the request, the facility verifies the person and approves it, and the Service runs the export or deletion. Guardians can act for their children. The target is to complete requests within 30 days of receipt. Marvy Labs will forward any request sent to us about End User data to the facility without undue delay and will not respond to it ourselves except to say we have passed it on.

9.3 For data we control (website visitors, staff accounts, billing contacts), email us at [TO CONFIRM: privacy contact email]. We will verify your identity, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

9.4 Marketing messages from a facility include an unsubscribe method, and facilities can record opt-outs and consent in the Service. Service messages (such as booking confirmations and security notices) cannot be opted out of while you have an account.

9.5 Some data is kept despite a deletion request where we or the facility must keep it by law, for accounting, or to defend legal claims.
