## backend/app/modules/reporting/README.md

- [contract] The contract tag `reporting` has `GET /dashboards/management` (`getManagementDashboard`, `docs/api/openapi-programs.yaml:6350`), but no route serves it (`backend/app/modules/reporting/router.py:25-109` has 15 routes) and nothing else in `backend/app` does. Its permission `reporting.dashboard.read` is registered but unused (`backend/app/modules/reporting/permissions.py:15`). The README's "Deviations from the contract" (lines 82-90) and "Not built" (lines 92-95) never mention the dashboard.
- [contract] Line 25 says "any family key (`reporting.<family>.run`) opens the centre". The router gate is `require(*service.RUN_KEYS)` (`backend/app/modules/reporting/router.py:21`), and `RUN_KEYS` is only the keys of registered specs (`backend/app/modules/reporting/service.py:27`): financial, membership, attendance, pos and time_clock (`registry.py:533-534`, `reports/money.py:471-558`). Holders of only `reporting.client.run`, `.program.run`, `.payroll.run`, `.league.run` or `.marketing.run` (`permissions.py:18,26,29,36,37`) are refused, because no report uses those keys.
- [stale-not-built] Line 94 lists "the reports outside the P0 set" as not built, yet `catalogue.py:7-9` now registers 17 more reports in `reports/money.py:465-588` and `reports/memberships.py:282-359` (`receivables`, `unapplied_payments`, `income_balances`, `underpaid_items`, `accrued_session_revenue`, `unused_session_value`, `package_summary`, `expiring_packages`, `renewal_candidates`, `package_sales`, `membership_revenue_by_month`, `deferred_membership_revenue`, `membership_actions`, `membership_attrition`, `expiring_memberships`, `membership_referrals`).
