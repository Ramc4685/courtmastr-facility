# Courtmastr Facility Children's Privacy Notice

DRAFT, not legal advice, pending attorney review. Last updated: 2026-11-13.

This notice explains how Courtmastr Facility, provided by Marvy Labs LLC ("Marvy Labs"), handles information about children. It supplements our Privacy Policy. Facilities that use the Service ("Customers") decide how children take part in their programs and are responsible for their own notices and consents. Several rules below are product design commitments. Where a rule is not yet built, it is marked and must be finished or the statement removed before launch.

## 1. Who this applies to

1.1 A "Child" means a person under 13. A "Minor" means a person under the age of majority the facility sets (default 18). A "Guardian" means a parent or legal guardian with authority over a Minor, recorded in the Service.

1.2 The Service is business software for facilities. Children use facilities through accounts that adults create. We do not offer the Service to children directly and we do not market to children.

## 2. Accounts for children

2.1 A Child's profile is created and managed by a Guardian from the Guardian's own signed-in account, or by facility staff who record the Guardian's authority. A Child does not have a login. 2.2 A Minor aged 13 or over may have their own login only if the facility turns that on and a Guardian with authority invites them. The setting cannot go below age 13. The facility configures this threshold. [TO CONFIRM: the minimum login age the product defaults to and whether any Customer may raise it; owner decision.]

2.3 If someone states that they are under the login age while signing up for themselves, the sign-up stops with a neutral message and what they typed is not kept. [TO CONFIRM: built and verified.]

2.4 An adult who asks to link themselves to an existing child's profile sees nothing and gets no access until facility staff approve. The claim does not reveal whether the profile exists.

## 3. What we collect about children

Only what the facility's forms require for the activity: usually name, date of birth, Guardian contact details, emergency contacts and any health or allergy information the facility asks for and marks sensitive. Optional fields are not prompted on children's forms. We do not collect a child's precise location, government identifiers, biometric templates or advertising identifiers.

## 4. How information about children is used

4.1 Only to run the facility's bookings, programs, memberships, check-in, safety and administration, and to meet legal duties.

4.2 No marketing to children. Notices and messages about a Child go to the Guardian. A Child receives no direct email, text or push notification. A Minor aged 13 or over who has a login may receive transactional messages, copied to the Guardian, and no marketing. [TO CONFIRM: built and verified; the readiness review found campaigns can still reach a child's own address.]

4.3 No behavioral advertising. We do not use children's data for advertising, profiling for advertising, or sale or sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising.

4.4 No third-party analytics or tracking in the web app or mobile app. If we add product analytics later it will be first-party and will exclude children's records.

4.5 A Child cannot buy, store a payment method or sign a document. A Guardian does those things.

## 5. What others can see

5.1 Children's names are not shown publicly by default. If a facility publishes league rosters, standings or galleries, a Child appears at most as a first name and last initial, and only where a Guardian has given media consent. No photos appear without consent. [TO CONFIRM: not yet built. The product currently prints full names on public league pages without an age check; this must be fixed before launch.]

5.2 A Child's record is never returned by kiosk name search. A Child checks in by credential or through the Guardian's app card. Children cannot use member-to-member contact features.

5.3 Staff access to a Child's sensitive fields is limited by permission and is recorded in the audit trail.

## 6. Parental rights

6.1 A Guardian can view, correct and export their Child's information, withdraw consent for each purpose (account, program registration, media, marketing, virtual sessions), and ask for deletion. These rights are exercised in the portal or through the facility. Withdrawing account consent starts a deletion request for the facility to approve.

6.2 Deletion removes personal fields and attached data, and keeps billing, attendance counts and accounting records in anonymized form. A signed waiver is retained under restriction for the period the facility sets because it may be needed to defend legal claims.

6.3 When a Minor reaches the age of majority, Guardian authority ends automatically, Guardians lose access, and the adult is invited to claim their own login.

## 7. COPPA-oriented practice (general terms)

7.1 The facility is the operator that decides whether to collect a Child's personal information and for what. Marvy Labs provides the Service and processes that information for the facility. The Customer must obtain any parental consent required for a Child and must give the notices the law requires.

7.2 The Service is built to support parental consent. It records a consent entry for each Guardian and purpose, with the method, time and network address, in a record that is added to and not overwritten. The method is recorded as one of: a card payment completed by the Guardian in the same registration, a signed consent or waiver accepted by the Guardian, or staff attestation for in-person sign-ups.

## 8. Retention for children

Children's information follows the same retention rules as other End User data (see the Privacy Policy), and the facility can delete it earlier. Facilities should delete children's data when it is no longer needed for the activity.

## 9. Security

Children's records are treated as sensitive: access is limited by permission, sensitive health values are encrypted in the database, and exports leave sensitive columns out unless the facility selects them and is permitted to.
