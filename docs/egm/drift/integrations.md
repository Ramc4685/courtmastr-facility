## backend/app/modules/integrations/README.md

- [wording] Line 17 says `jobs.py` has "an unscheduled sweep", but line 70 says `sweep_webhook_deliveries` runs every minute. The scheduler does enqueue it (`backend/app/scheduler.py:20`, `EVERY_MINUTE`), so line 17 is stale. The actor's docstring makes the same stale claim: "nothing schedules this yet" (`backend/app/modules/integrations/jobs.py:21-22`).
- [contract] Line 18 says `router.py` holds "the endpoints in `docs/api/openapi-programs.yaml` tag integrations". The contract has 38 integrations operations (docs/api/openapi-programs.yaml:6361-7115); the router serves 18 of them (`backend/app/modules/integrations/router.py:33-153`, counting `/api-keys`, `/webhook-event-types`, `/webhook-endpoints`, `/webhook-deliveries` and three `/external-connections` routes). The earlier "22 of 38" figure was wrong. The 20 unserved ones are `getExternalConnection`, `updateExternalConnection`, `runSync`, `retryAccountingExport`, the QuickBooks and Zoom receivers, the OAuth callback, sync mappings, session video meetings, the portal join, and the five calendar-feed operations.
- [undocumented] Of those unserved operations, these are not in the "Not built" list (lines 73-75): `getExternalConnection` (GET `/external-connections/{connection_id}`), `updateExternalConnection` (PATCH), `runSync` (POST `/external-connections/{connection_id}/sync`), `retryAccountingExport` (POST `/accounting-exports/{accounting_export_id}/retry`), `receiveQuickBooksEvents` (POST `/webhooks/quickbooks`) and `receiveZoomEvents` (POST `/webhooks/zoom`) (openapi-programs.yaml:6779, 6794, 6920, 6949, 6967, 7115).
- [contract] The router serves five operations with no contract entry: `GET /accounting-exports` (`backend/app/modules/integrations/router.py:155`), `POST /accounting-exports` (:161), `GET /integration-adapters` (:169), `GET /api-reference` (:175) and `GET /adapter-commands` (:183). The contract lists accounting exports only as `GET /external-connections/{connection_id}/accounting-exports` (openapi-programs.yaml:6883), which the router does not serve. README line 19 documents `GET /api-reference`, but the contract does not.

The QuickBooks and Zoom receivers are among the unserved contract operations above.
